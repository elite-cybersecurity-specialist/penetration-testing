# Frequently Asked Questions

[← Back to main page](../README.md)

### What is the difference between vulnerability scanning and penetration testing?

Scanning can identify possible weaknesses. A scoped penetration test places findings into context, uses authorized validation where appropriate, documents potential impact and recommends improvements. Scanning may be one component of the work.

### Is blackbox testing always the best approach?

Not always. Blackbox testing can examine the external perspective; graybox can expose user-privilege issues; whitebox can review deeper design and implementation assumptions. The right combination depends on the customer.

### Can ECS assess my website, mobile app, servers and network in a single engagement?

Potentially, where the technologies, availability and authorization permit it. Each component and method must be included in the agreed scope; combined-scope assessments require coordination and may have different timelines.

### Do you provide remediation and re-testing?

Remediation recommendations and final re-testing are part of the ECS methodology. The specific implementation services, responsibilities and re-test allowance must be stated in the engagement agreement.

### Can you guarantee that my environment is impossible to compromise?

No responsible assessment can provide that absolute guarantee. The goal is to identify and reduce significant risks, verify controls and communicate residual exposure and assessment limitations.

### Can you test production systems without interrupting business?

Testing may be planned to minimize disruption, but no responsible assessor can promise zero operational impact in every environment. We establish system protections, test windows, prohibited actions and stop conditions in advance.

### Will you publish our vulnerabilities on GitHub?

Not without an explicit approved disclosure arrangement. This repository documents public-facing methodology and services, not confidential client findings.

### Do you provide social engineering, phishing and physical security testing?

These are listed ECS service areas, but each requires appropriate specialized scoping, authorization and safety controls. They are not automatically included in technical penetration testing.

### Does referencing OWASP, NIST, CIS or DISA mean the customer receives certification?

No. The frameworks can inform testing and improvement, but a formal certification or compliance audit is a distinct process with separate requirements.
